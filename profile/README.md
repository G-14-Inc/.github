# G-14, Inc.

Our work connects research, standards, and verification at the boundary between what an AI system proposes and what takes effect.

### Our work

| Research | ECL-1 | Verification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Investigating how AI action can be bounded, evaluated, and independently reviewed. | An open standard for external control of AI that acts. | Building the schemas, tests, and tools that make control claims inspectable. |

### The questions behind the work

- **Authority:** Who authorized this action, and within what limits?
- **Enforcement:** What prevents an action outside those limits?
- **Evidence:** What can an independent reviewer verify afterward?

Research and standard releases will appear here as they are published.
